Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Last Word Letters is operated by Morounke Williams-Tobi, based in the United Kingdom. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

By using this website and our services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

What we collect

When you use Last Word Letters, we collect the following:

  • Your email address — provided when you place an order or request a free letter opening. Used to deliver your letter and, with your consent, to send you occasional updates about our services.
  • Your answers to our intake questions — the personal details, memories, and context you share when writing your letter. These are used solely to generate your letter and are not stored beyond what is necessary to complete your order.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see, store, or have access to your card details.
  • Basic usage data — via Google Analytics, we collect anonymised data about how visitors use the site (pages visited, time on site). No personal information is attached to this data.

How we use your information

  • To generate and deliver your personalised letter
  • To process your payment securely via Stripe
  • To send your letter to your inbox via our email delivery partner, Resend
  • To respond to any questions or requests you send us
  • To send occasional updates about new services — only where you have given us permission to do so

We do not use your personal information for any other purpose. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third parties we work with

To operate our service we use the following trusted third-party providers:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Your card details are handled directly by Stripe and are never accessible to us. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Resend — email delivery. Your email address and letter are passed to Resend solely to deliver your letter to your inbox.
  • Anthropic — AI letter generation. Your answers to our intake questions are passed to Anthropic's API to generate your letter. This data is processed in accordance with Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Vercel — website hosting. Our site is hosted on Vercel's servers.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised site usage data only.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

Data retention

We retain your email address for as long as necessary to deliver your order and, where applicable, to send service updates. Your intake answers are used to generate your letter and are not retained beyond what is needed to complete your order.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for Google Analytics (anonymised usage tracking) and Stripe (payment processing). By using this site you consent to these cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect the functionality of the site.

Questions about this policy?

Contact us at hello@lastwordletters.com and we will respond within 5 working days.
